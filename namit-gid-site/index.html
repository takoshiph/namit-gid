Here’s our story

At my husband's family table in Romblon, a good dish started a chain: one bite, one "Namit gid," then everyone turning to each other, nodding, saying it again. So good you say it twice. One day I caught myself doing it too.

It stuck, and it's the only review I cook for. So now I cook hoping somebody at your table says it. So don't be shy, say it out loud, "Namit gid." You never know, the one beside you might know it too.
