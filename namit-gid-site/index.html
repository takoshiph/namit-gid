Namit gid? Tell us, your kind words help this little kitchen grow.

Reviews are checked before they show up. Salamat gid!

At my husband's family table in Romblon, a good dish started a chain: one bite, one "Namit gid," then everyone turning to each other, nodding, saying it again. So good you say it twice. One day I caught myself doing it too.

It stuck, and it's the only review I cook for. Every plate I send out is meant to remind you what good food feels like. So don't be shy, say it out loud, "Namit gid." You never know, the one beside you might know it too.
